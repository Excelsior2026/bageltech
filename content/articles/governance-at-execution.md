## The gap
Most teams define governance upstream (training) and downstream (audits). But production risk appears in the middle: at decision time.

## What execution governance adds
- Context-aware checks before action
- Authority boundaries based on actor and scope
- Safe escalation when confidence is low

## Outcome
You get fewer high-confidence wrong actions and faster incident reviews because every decision path is explicit.
